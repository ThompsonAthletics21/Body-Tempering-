Is tempering right for you?

Tempering isn't recommended during pregnancy, if you take blood thinners, or if you have osteoporosis, varicose veins, or a recent injury in the area being worked. Let me know about any injuries or medical conditions before your first session, and check with your doctor if you're unsure.
